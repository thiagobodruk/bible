DISCLAIMER — Russian Synodal Translation (SYNOD)

This file (synod.json / synod.xml) contains 64 of the 66 canonical books.

The Book of Esther and the Book of Daniel are intentionally MISSING from
this dataset.

Reason: the source site for this scraper does not publish standalone
texts for these two books in the Synodal Translation (SYNOD). The site
explicitly states, for each: "A versão SYNOD não inclui o livro de
Ester/Daniel" ("The SYNOD version does not include the Book of
Esther/Daniel"). Historically, the Russian Synodal Bible presents both
books in their expanded Greek form (interleaved with deuterocanonical
additions) rather than as separate, independently addressable books —
the site links to the alternate text under "Ester Grego" ("Esther,
Greek") instead of the regular book list, and that content does not fit
this project's per-book chapter/verse format. Daniel appears to be
affected the same way, though the site does not expose an equivalent
"Daniel Grego" link for it — attempting to fetch it simply returns
"indisponível na versão SYNOD" ("unavailable in the SYNOD version").

SYNOD is the only Russian-language translation currently available from
the source, so no alternative could be substituted for either missing
book.

All other 64 books were downloaded normally and are complete.
